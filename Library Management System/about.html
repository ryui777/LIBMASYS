About PTCHub

A digital platform designed to simplify library operations and improve access to educational resources for PTC students and staff.

The PTCHub Library Management System was developed to help students and staff efficiently manage library transactions such as borrowing, returning, and tracking books. It replaces manual processes with a faster and more organized digital system.


This system aims to improve accessibility, reduce errors in record keeping, and provide a smooth experience for both users and librarians.

Key Features

  • ?? Book catalog browsing and search
  • ?? Borrow and return management
  • ?? User account tracking
  • ? Due date monitoring
  • ?? Library record management for admins

Our Mission

To develop an efficient Library Management System that simplifies borrowing, returning, and tracking of library resources for students and staff of PTC.

Our Vision

To become a reliable digital library platform that promotes easy access to knowledge and supports academic excellence through modern technology.
